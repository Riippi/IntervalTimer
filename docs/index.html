Privacy Policy — Interval Timer X

Last updated: September 2026

Interval Timer X (package name org.leeko.intervaltimer) is an offline interval-timer app for Android. This page explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Summary

Interval Timer X does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no account system, and no third-party integrations of any kind. Everything the app does happens entirely on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your workout presets (names, round counts, timing) are saved locally in a private database on your device, using Android's standard app storage. This data is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Permissions the app requests

  • Vibrate — used to give haptic feedback when a round starts or ends.
  • Wake lock — used to keep the screen/timer running reliably during a workout.
  • Read phone state — used only to detect an incoming call so the timer can pause automatically. The app does not read, store, or transmit your phone number, call log, or any call content. This permission is optional to the app's core function; if you decline it, the app works normally except it will not auto-pause during a call.

Third parties

Interval Timer X does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, and does not share data with anyone because it does not collect any.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
